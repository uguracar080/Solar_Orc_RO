SOLAR-ORC-RO SISTEM MODELI - PROJE TEKNIK ACIKLAMA DOSYASI
===========================================================

Guncelleme tarihi: 2026-08-25
Ana amac: Bu dosya, projeyi yeni okuyacak bir yazilimcinin tum klasoru
yeniden taramadan aktif sistem mimarisini, hesap akisini, parametrelerin
nerede nasil kullanildigini ve karar algoritmalarini anlayabilmesi icin
tutulan canli teknik dokumandir. Projede hesap mantigi veya arayuz degistikce
ilgili bolum bu dosyada da guncellenmelidir.


1. KISA OZET
------------

Bu proje entegre bir solar termal - ORC - RO deniz suyu aritma sisteminin
MATLAB modelidir. Aktif V1 hedefi optimizasyon degil, tek bir sabit
konfigurasyonun yillik/saatlik olarak uc uca calismasidir. Optimizasyon
daha sonra bu sabit-konfigurasyon kosucusunu aday degerlendirme fonksiyonu
gibi cagiracaktir.

Aktif ana zincir:

Weather + seawater profile
  -> PTC solar field
  -> ORC evaporator hot stream
  -> fixed-geometry ORC off-design dispatch
  -> ORC condenser cooling-water outlet
  -> RO train dispatch with seawater preheater control
  -> RO ANN surrogate
  -> cooling tower trim/backup heat rejection
  -> auxiliary pump electrical bookkeeping
  -> hourly outputs, summary, report, figures

Aktif ana giris:

  System_Driver/run_single_configuration_v1.m

Aktif programatik API:

  System_Driver/run_single_configuration_core_v1.m

Aktif saatlik kapali cevrim cozumcu:

  System_Driver/system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1.m

Eski sirali dispatcher:

  System_Driver/system_hourly_dispatch_v1.m

Bu dosya hala repoda bulunur fakat aktif run path artik kapali cevrim
cozumcudur. Yeni entegrasyonlar once system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1.m
uzerinden dusunulmelidir.

3. CALISTIRMA VE ANA AKIS
-------------------------

3.1 Script akisi

run_single_configuration_v1.m MATLAB Run butonu icin hazirlanmis script
girisidir. Is akisi:

1. ProjectRoot bulunur.
2. system_paths_v1(ProjectRoot) cagrilir.
3. cfg = system_config_v1(ProjectRoot) ile varsayilan config alinir.
4. Script icindeki kullanici/editlenebilir bloklar cfg uzerine override
   edilir.
5. Results = run_single_configuration_core_v1(cfg) cagrilir.

Scriptte su an run_single_configuration_v1.m icin:

  cfg.sim.startHour = 770
  cfg.sim.endHour = 800
  cfg.sim.timeStep_min = 60

Bu script penceresi 30 saatlik bir test/konfigurasyon kosusudur.
system_config_v1.m default'u ise startHour=0, endHour=24, timeStep_min=60
olarak tutulur. startHour/endHour 0-based ve half-open interval mantigindadir.
Ornek: 5500-5548 araligi 48 saat kosar. timeStep_min 60'tan kucukse
sayisal weather/seawater kolonlari lineer interpolasyonla uretilir.

3.2 Core fonksiyon akisi

run_single_configuration_core_v1.m sirasi:

1. Path ayari:
   system_paths_v1(ProjectRoot)

2. Simulation/output config finalizasyonu:
   localFinalizeSimulationConfig
   localFinalizeOutputConfig

3. Girdi okuma:
   localLoadWeather(cfg)
   readtable(cfg.paths.seawater_csv)
   localPrepareInputWindow

4. ORC Stage-1 tasarim:
   localDesignOrc
   -> orc_finalize_config
   -> orc_stage1_sizing

5. Preheater ve cooling tower tasarim:
   localDesignHeatRejectionTrain
   -> preheater_sthe_design_v1
   -> ct_default_config
   -> ct_rate_design_point

6. Saatlik kapali cevrim sistem cozumu:
   system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1

7. Summary:
   localSummarize
   localAttachRunTiming

8. Figurler:
   generate_all_figures_v1

9. Ciktilar:
   writetable(Hourly, hourly_csv)
   writetable(Summary, summary_csv)
   write_simulation_report_v1
   save(summary_mat, cfg, Summary, Hourly, PHDesign, CTDesign, orcDesign,
        orcAnnual, FigureResults, ReportFile, RunTiming)

7. SAATLIK KAPALI CEVRIM COZUCU
-------------------------------

system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1.m her zaman adiminda iki sicaklik dongusunu
kapatir:

1. Solar/ORC evaporator loop:
   Solar field inlet TsolarIn_K tahmin edilir.
   Solar field bu inlet ile T_HTF_out_K uretir.
   ORC evaporator bu hot stream'i kullanir.
   ORC evaporator hot-side outlet TsolarNextRaw_K olarak gelir.
   Storage return sink aktifse TsolarNext_K, TsolarIn_K degerine cekilebilir.
   Residual:
     solar_loop_residual_K = TsolarNext_K - TsolarIn_K

2. Condenser/preheater/CT loop:
   ORC condenser cold-water inlet TcwIn_C tahmin edilir.
   ORC condenser outlet TcwCondOut_C olur.
   Preheater varsa bu suyu sogutur ve RO feed'i isitir.
   CT gerekirse kalan sicakligi hedef T_cw_target_C civarina dusurur.
   CT outlet TcwNext_C bir sonraki loop tahminidir.
   Residual:
     cw_loop_residual_K = TcwNext_C - TcwIn_C

Ic iterasyon:

  for iter = 1:cfg.coupling.max_iter
      localSolveOneCouplingIteration(...)
      if abs(solar_residual) <= tol_solar_K ve
         abs(cw_residual) <= tol_cw_K:
         converged = true
         break
      degilse:
         TsolarIn_K = relax(old, next)
         TcwIn_C = relax(old, next)
  end

Varsayilan:

  max_iter = 12
  tol_solar_K = 1.0
  tol_cw_K = 0.25
  relax = 1.0

Saat sonrasi warm-start:

  TsolarGuess_K = onceki TsolarNext_K
  TcwGuess_C = onceki TcwNext_C

8. SOLAR FIELD HESAPLARI
------------------------

Aktif dosya:

  Solar_Field/solar_field_ptc_v1.m

Amac:
  Eski monolitik PTC kodundan ayrilmis PTC-only modeldir. ORC, condenser,
  exergy ve plotting katmani yoktur.

Girdi struct:

  SolarInput.DNI_Wm2
  SolarInput.T_amb_C veya T_amb_K
  SolarInput.WindSpd_ms
  SolarInput.T_HTF_in_K

  SolarInput.solver_cache optional

Config:

  SolarConfig.min_DNI_Wm2
  SolarConfig.min_useful_heat_W
  SolarConfig.T_HTF_in_K
  SolarConfig.V_Lmin_1module
  SolarConfig.field.N_series
  SolarConfig.field.N_parallel
  SolarConfig.ptc.*
  SolarConfig.Nusselt_multiplier
  SolarConfig.sigma

PTC geometri varsayilanlari:

  W = 5.0 m
  L = 7.8 m
  Aa = 39.0 m2
  Dri = 66 mm
  Dro = 70 mm
  Dci = 109 mm
  Dco = 115 mm
  eps_c = 0.86
  r_mirror = 0.83
  tau_cover = 0.95
  alpha_abs = 0.96
  gamma_int = 1.00
  K_theta = 1.0
  eta_opt = r_mirror*tau_cover*alpha_abs*gamma_int*K_theta

Seri/paralel mantik:

  Ns = field.N_series
  Np = field.N_parallel

Her paralel kolda Ns adet modul seri cozulur. Tek koldaki useful heat
toplanir, toplam field heat Np ile carpilir. Pressure drop seri moduller
boyunca toplanir; paralel kollarda ayni string dP kabul edilir.

Tek modul fsolve bilinmeyenleri:

  Tr   absorber/receiver sicakligi [K]
  Tc   cover sicakligi [K]
  Tout HTF outlet sicakligi [K]

Residual denklemleri:

  F1 = QlossR2C - QlossC2A
  F2 = Qu - QuConv
  F3 = Qabs - Qu - QlossR2C

Temel ifadeler:

  Qs = Aa * Gb
  Qabs = Qs * eta_opt
  Qu = mdot * cp * (Tout - Tin)
  Re = 4*mdot/(pi*Dri*mu)
  Pr = mu*cp/k
  Nu0 = 0.023*Re^0.8*Pr^0.4
  Nu = Nusselt_multiplier * Nu0
  h = Nu*k/Dri
  QuConv = h*Ari*(Tr - Tfm)
  Tfm = 0.5*(Tin + Tout)
  eps_r = 0.05599 + 1.039e-4*Tr + 2.249e-7*Tr^2
  Tsky = 0.0553*Tamb^1.5
  hout = 4*Vwind^0.58*Dco^(-0.42)
  QlossR2C = Aro*sigma*(Tr^4 - Tc^4) /
             ((1/eps_r) + ((1-eps_c)/eps_c)*(Aro/Aci))
  QlossC2A = Aco*hout*(Tc-Tamb) + Aco*sigma*eps_c*(Tc^4-Tsky^4)

HTF:

  Syltherm800 property hesabi ortak ORC_v23/syltherm800_properties_v2.m
  uzerinden yapilir. Bu fonksiyon persistent cache ile rho(T), cp(T),
  mu(T), k(T), h(T) ve T(h) interpolantlarini bir kez hazirlar. Solar alan
  ve ORC stream property hesaplari ayni tabloyu kullanir.

  Eski davranista h(T) her cagrida 80 nokta cp integrali ile, T(h) ise
  fzero + tekrarli h(T) residual degerlendirmesi ile cozulebiliyordu. Aktif
  davranista h(T) ve T(h) tek interpolasyon cagrisi ile gelir.

Pressure drop:

  V_m3s = V_Lmin_1module*1e-3/60
  mdot = rho*V_m3s
  u = mdot/(rho*Aflow)
  fr = 1/(0.79*log(Re)-1.64)^2
  dP = fr*(L/Dri)*(0.5*rho*u^2)

Solar output:

  Q_useful_W
  T_HTF_in_K
  T_HTF_out_K
  mdot_HTF_kg_s
  dP_HTF_Pa
  aperture_area_m2
  N_series
  N_parallel
  ptc_field
  solver_cache
  feasible
  status

Solar off:

  DNI < min_DNI_Wm2 ise Q_useful_W=0, mdot=0, T_out=T_in,
  feasible=false, status=SOLAR_OFF.


9. SOLAR FLOW CONTROL KARAR ALGORITMASI
---------------------------------------

Flow control system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1.m icindedir:

  localSelectSolarOperatingPoint

Adaylar:

  factors = unique([cfg.solar.flow_control.factor_list, 1.0])
  V_Lmin_1module = Vref * factor

Adaptive local search:

  Eger onceki saat gecerlidir ve DNI/Tin degisimi kucukse sadece son
  secilen factor cevresindeki local_neighbor_steps kadar komsu denenir.

Full search'e dusme nedenleri:

  - Secilen local aday local araligin kenarindaysa
  - Secilen aday feasible degilse
  - Tout max limite veya min banda cok yakinsa
  - Weather/Tin degisimi buyukse
  - Search state gecersizse

Adaptive fallback optimizasyonu:

  Local search yapildiktan sonra full search gerekirse, local search'te
  zaten cozulmus factor adaylari tekrar cozulmez. Full search yalniz eksik
  factor listesini cozer ve local/full candidate listeleri birlestirilir.

Aday feasible kosulu:

  deltaT_K >= min_deltaT_gain_K
  ToutC <= T_out_max_C

Secim:

  selection_mode closest_target ise hedefe en yakin Tout secilir.
  Aksi halde max_heat_below_limit mantigi kullanilir:
    Quseful yuksek, hedeften sapmasi kucuk aday tercih edilir.

Guard:

  ToutC > T_out_max_C ise SolarOut infeasible, status TEMP_LIMIT_HIGH.
  deltaT_K < min_deltaT_gain_K ise status LOW_SOLAR_GAIN.

Saatlik output diagnostikleri:

10. ORC STAGE-1 TASARIM
-----------------------

Ana dosya:

  ORC_v23/orc_stage1_sizing.m

Stage-1 nominal ORC termodinamik halini cozer ve evaporator/condenser STHE
trenlerini boyutlandirir. Donen geometri Stage-2 icin frozen kabul edilir.

Termodinamik cycle:

  ORC_v23/orc_thermo_design.m

Zorunlu girdiler:

  systemInput.orc_P_evap_des
  systemInput.orc_P_cond_des

Boyut spec:

  systemInput.orc_W_nom veya systemInput.orc_mdot_des

Hidrolik dP girdileri:

  orcHydraulic.orc_dp_evap_wf
  orcHydraulic.orc_dp_cond_wf

Basinc tanimlari:

  P_orcevap = turbine inlet / evaporator outlet pressure
  P_orccond = pump inlet / condenser outlet pressure
  P_orcpump_out = P_orcevap + dp_evap_wf
  P_orcturb_out = P_orccond + dp_cond_wf

Cycle kabulleri:

  turbine inlet mode = sat_vapor
  condenser outlet mode = sat_liquid
  subcritical ORC

Temel hesaplar:

  h_pump_in = h(P_cond, Q=0)
  s_pump_in = s(P_cond, Q=0)
  h_pump_out_s = h(P_pump_out, s_pump_in)
  h_pump_out = h_pump_in + (h_pump_out_s-h_pump_in)/eta_pump

  h_turb_in = h(P_evap, Q=1)
  s_turb_in = s(P_evap, Q=1)
  h_turb_out_s = h(P_turb_out, s_turb_in)
  h_turb_out = h_turb_in - eta_turb*(h_turb_in-h_turb_out_s)

  w_turb = h_turb_in - h_turb_out
  w_pump = h_pump_out - h_pump_in
  w_net = w_turb - w_pump
  q_evap = h_turb_in - h_pump_out
  q_cond = h_turb_out - h_pump_in

  mdot_orc = W_nom / w_net  veya verilen mdot
  W_orcnet = mdot_orc*w_net
  Q_orcevap = mdot_orc*q_evap
  Q_orccond = mdot_orc*q_cond
  eta_orc_th = W_orcnet/Q_orcevap

Energy balance:

  Q_orcevap - W_orcnet - Q_orccond

Hydraulic feedback:

  orc_hydraulic_feedback.m aktifse Stage-1 boyunca su adimlari tekrarlar:
    1. Mevcut evaporator/condenser wf dP tahminiyle thermo solve
    2. Evaporator ve condenser STHE yeniden size edilir
    3. Hesaplanan wf dP degerleri under-relaxation ile yeni tahmin olur
    4. relChange <= orc_tol_hydraulic_rel olunca convergence
    5. Final pass ve relaxed finalRel check yapilir

12. ORC STAGE-2 / OFF-DESIGN DISPATCH
-------------------------------------

Aktif saatlik sistemde ORC cagrisi:

  orc_offdesign_dispatch(orcDesign, hotStream, coldStream, op, orcConfigStep)

Eger cfg.orc.operating_mode dispatch degilse:

  orc_offdesign_rating(...)

offdesign_rating:

  Frozen geometry zorunludur. Her operating point icin:
    1. P_evap, P_cond, mdot_orc veya W_nom alinir
    2. Thermo solve
    3. Fixed-geometry evaporator rating
    4. Fixed-geometry condenser rating
    5. Working-fluid dP fixed-point iteration
    6. feasible = hydraulic converged + evap feasible + cond feasible + energy balance OK

Hata korumasi:

  config.orc_stage2_returnInfeasibleOnError true ise property/numerical hata
  yillik kosuyu durdurmaz, guarded-infeasible step dondurur.

dispatch algoritmasi:

  ORC_v23/orc_offdesign_dispatch.m

Amac:
  Sabit geometri icin feasible ve net gucu pozitif tek operating point bulmak.

Candidate building:

  Pev0 = design veya warm/current P_evap
  Pco0 = design veya warm/current P_cond
  md0  = design veya warm/current mdot

Candidate'lar mdotFactor, PevapFactor, PcondFactor kombinasyonlaridir.

Stress-aware ordering:

  sourceStress:
    Hot stream T_in design'dan dusuk veya mdot dusukse.

  sinkStress:
    Cold stream T_in design'dan yuksek veya mdot dusukse.

  sourceStress varsa low-Pevap adaylari erken denenir.
  sinkStress varsa high-Pcond adaylari erken denenir.
  combo stress varsa her iki rahatlatma birlikte denenir.

Early coverage tier:

  orc_dispatch_coverageMdotFactors ile moderate part-load adaylari erken
  araya sokulur.

Warm start:

  Onceki feasible ORC step'in P_evap, P_cond, mdot ve stream metadata'si
  kaydedilir. Dis boundary benzerse warm-start candidate ikinci siraya
  alinir. Benzerlik limitleri:
    orc_stage2_dispatch_warmStart_Ttol_K
    orc_stage2_dispatch_warmStart_mdotRelTol

Prescreen:

  localThermoPrescreen once ucuz thermo ve kapasite kontrolu yapar.

  Fail status ornekleri:
    prescreen-low-power
    prescreen-source-cold
    prescreen-sink-hot
    prescreen-hot-capacity
    prescreen-cold-capacity
    prescreen-guarded

Capacity prescreen:

  Hot stream entalpi kapasitesi:
    Q_hot_cap = mdot_hot*(h_hot_in-h_hot_limit)*tolerance

  Cold stream entalpi kapasitesi:
    Q_cold_cap = mdot_cold*(h_cold_limit-h_cold_in)*tolerance

  ORC Q_evap veya Q_cond bu kapasiteyi asarsa aday rating'e gitmeden elenir.

Fast screening:

  orc_dispatch_fastScreening true ise once az iterasyonlu HX rating yapilir.
  Feasible gorunen secili aday sonra exact validation ile yeniden rating edilir.

Preheater guard:

  cfg.preheater.enforce_cond_out_above_feed true ise
  orc_dispatch_preheaterGuardEnabled aktiflesir ve condenser outlet sicakligi
  feed+margin altinda kalan ORC adaylari reddedilebilir.

Selected status:

  Feasible aday bulunursa:
    orc_status = dispatch-on

  Bulunamazsa:
    orc_status = dispatch-off
    orc_dispatch.candidateLog nedenleri saklar.

Sistem cozumcu bu loglardan orc_off_reason kolonunu turetir:

  ON
  SOLAR_OFF
  SOURCE_TOO_COLD
  SINK_TOO_HOT
  HOT_STREAM_CAPACITY
  COLD_STREAM_CAPACITY
  MIN_POWER_OR_LOW_NET_WORK
  PREHEATER_COND_OUT_GUARD
  NUMERICAL_OR_PROPERTY_GUARD
  EVAPORATOR_PINCH_LIMIT
  CONDENSER_PINCH_LIMIT
  EVAPORATOR_AREA_LIMIT
  CONDENSER_AREA_LIMIT
  NO_FEASIBLE_DISPATCH_CANDIDATE

Diagnostic not:

  system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1 localOrcDispatchDiagnostics, ORC feasible
  yani dispatch-on saatlerinde de orc_dispatch candidate log'unu okur.
  Bu nedenle orc_dispatch_n_candidates_available,
  orc_dispatch_n_candidates_evaluated ve orc_dispatch_best_* kolonlari
  yalniz ORC_OFF saatleri icin degil, ON saatleri icin de dolabilir.


13. ORC HOT STREAM VE PROPERTY BACKEND
--------------------------------------

Aktif default:

  cfg.orc.hot_stream_fluid = 'Syltherm800'

  C / cp
  V / viscosity

15. PREHEATER TASARIMI
----------------------

Aktif design:

  Heat_Exchangers/preheater_sthe_design_v1.m

Amac:

  ORC condenser cooling-water outlet'indeki isiyi, RO feed deniz suyuna
  aktarmak. Iki akis fiziksel olarak ayridir.

Konvansiyon:

  Hot side/shell: ORC condenser-loop water
  Cold side/tube: seawater

Design girdileri localDesignHeatRejectionTrain icinde kurulur:

  T_hot_in_C = design condenser-water outlet
  T_cold_in_C = cfg.preheater.T_sw_design_C
  T_cold_out_target_C = localPreheaterTargetC(cfg,TswDesign)
  mdot_hot_kg_s = cfg.orc.mdot_cw_design_kg_s
  mdot_cold_kg_s = N_train_total * Qf_train_m3h * rho_seawater / 3600

Not:
  T_sw_design_C finite degilse, geriye uyumluluk icin ilk seawater profili
  sicakligi fallback olarak kullanilir. Normal aktif varsayilan 25 C'dir.

Design condenser-water outlet yaklasimi:

  Qcond = abs(orcDesign.orc_thermo.Q_orccond)
  TcwHotC = TcwInC + Qcond/(mdot_cw_design*cp_hot)

Target clip:

  TcOutRequested = min(input target, T_cold_out_max_C)
  TcOutTarget = min(TcOutRequested, ThIn - dT_min_K)

Eger target approach ile kisitlanirsa:

  target_limited_by_approach = true
  status = TARGET_APPROACH_LIMITED

Q design:

  Qtotal = mdotCold * cp_cold * (TcOutTarget - TcIn)

Hot outlet:

  ThOut = ThIn - Qtotal/(mdotHot*cp_hot)

Approach kontrolu:

  dT1 = ThIn - TcOutTarget
  dT2 = ThOut - TcIn
  min(dT1,dT2) >= dT_min_K olmalidir.

LMTD:

  L = (DT1-DT2)/log(DT1/DT2)

Geometri aramasi:

  Npar = 1..N_parallel_max
  Nt = Nt_list
  once Npar icinde en dusuk alanli feasible candidate aranir,
  ilk feasible Npar bulunduysa daha buyuk Npar'a gecilmeden kabul edilir.

Candidate:

  preheater_sthe_core_v1('geometry', geometry_family, Nt, NaN, config)
  shell_singlephase ve tube_singlephase ile h hesaplanir.
  U = 1/(1/hShell + 1/hTube)
  Areq = (Qtotal/Npar)/(U*LMTD)
  LtubeReq = Areq/(pi*do*Nt)
  Ltube = LtubeReq*(1 + designAreaMargin)

Limitler:

  Ltube min/max
  Dshell min/max
  pitch ratio
  do/Dshell
  baffle spacing

Design output:

  Q_design_W
  A_design_m2
  A_required_m2
  UA_design_WK
  U_design_Wm2K
  N_parallel
  Nt
  Ltube_m
  Dshell_m
  geometry
  dp_hot_Pa
  dp_cold_Pa
  shell
  tube


16. PREHEATER RUNTIME RATING VE KONTROL
---------------------------------------

Control:

  system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1 localRunPreheaterControl

Deadband:

  dTAvailable = TcwHotC - TswRawC

  dTAvailable >= deltaT_ON_K:
    preheater on

  dTAvailable <= deltaT_OFF_K:
    preheater off

  arada:
    onceki saat PHWasOn korunur

Runtime rating:

  preheater_sthe_rating_v1.m

Girdiler:

  T_hot_in_C
  T_cold_in_C
  mdot_hot_kg_s
  mdot_cold_kg_s
  Q_available_W
  T_cold_out_target_C
  PHDesign frozen geometry

Eger fixed geometry yoksa:

  status = NO_FIXED_GEOMETRY
  heat recovered = 0

Eger thermal driving force yoksa:

  ThIn <= TcIn + dT_min_K
  status = NO_THERMAL_DRIVING_FORCE

Effectiveness-NTU:

  U = 1/(1/hShell + 1/hTube)
  UA = U*A_design
  C_hot = mdotHot*cp_hot
  C_cold = mdotCold*cp_cold
  Cmin = min(C_hot,C_cold)
  Cmax = max(C_hot,C_cold)
  Cr = Cmin/Cmax
  NTU = UA/Cmin

Counterflow effectiveness:

  Cr ~= 1:
    eps = (1-exp(-NTU*(1-Cr))) /
          (1-Cr*exp(-NTU*(1-Cr)))

  Cr == 1:
    eps = NTU/(1+NTU)

Heat limits:

  Qntu = eps*Cmin*(ThIn-TcIn)
  Qupper = min(Qntu, Q_available_W)
  Qupper = min(Qupper, C_cold*(TcOutTarget-TcIn))
  Qupper = max(0,Qupper)

Approach-limited heat:

  Binary search yoktur. Hot outlet ve cold outlet terminal approach
  kisitlari analitik olarak ayni anda uygulanir:

    Qapproach_max = max(0, min(C_hot,C_cold)*(ThIn - TcIn - dT_min_K))
    Q = min(Qupper, Qapproach_max)

Output:

  Q_recovered_W
  T_hot_out_C / T_cw_out_C
  T_sw_out_C / T_RO_in_C
  effectiveness
  NTU
  U_runtime_Wm2K
  UA_runtime_WK
  dp_hot_Pa
  dp_cold_Pa
  feasible
  status

-----------------------

kopyasidir. Preheater runtime ORC helper fonksiyonlarini dogrudan cagirmasin

Action arayuzu:

  'default_config'
  'geometry'
  'tube_singlephase'
  'shell_singlephase'

Desteklenen geometri:

  one shell pass
  one tube pass
  60 degree layout
  condenser veya evaporator tube family

Tube single-phase:

  Re < 2300 ise Nu=3.66, fFanning=16/Re
  Re >= 2300 ise Gnielinski benzeri Nu ve Blasius/benzeri friction kullanilir
  dp = fDarcy*(L/di)*rho*v^2/2

Shell single-phase:

  Bell-Delaware benzeri ideal tube-bank heat transfer ve correction factor
  yapisi kullanilir. Jc, Jl, Jb, Js, Jr ve dp correction factorleri hesaplanir.
  Sealing strips ve equal baffle spacing baseline olarak Jb=fb=1, Js=fs=1.

  1. Ttarget >= Tin ise bypass, fan_ratio=0.
  2. fan_ratio_max ile ct_offdesign_merkel denenir.

18.1 COOLING-WATER VE SEAWATER AUX PUMPS

Aktif fonksiyon:

  system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1 localApplyRoAuxiliaryPumps

CW circulation pump:

  Su hattinda ORC condenser + preheater hot side + CT dolasir.
  Cooling tower ic pressure drop su an ihmal edilir.

  dP_cw_condenser_Pa = orc_condenser_rate.dp_orccw
  dP_cw_preheater_hot_Pa = PHOut.dp_hot_Pa, yalniz preheater_on true iken
  dP_cw_loop_Pa = dP_cw_condenser_Pa + dP_cw_preheater_hot_Pa

  W_cw_pump_W = mdot_cw_design_kg_s*dP_cw_loop_Pa /
                (rho_water*eta_hydraulic*eta_motor)

Seawater feed pump:

  Mevcut V1'de yalniz preheater cold-side dP'sini temsil eder.

  dP_sw_preheater_cold_Pa = PHOut.dp_cold_Pa, yalniz preheater_on true iken

                     (rho_seawater*eta_hydraulic*eta_motor)

RO elektrik butcesi:

  W_available_for_RO_W = W_ORC_net_W
                       - W_solar_pump_W
                       - W_CT_fan_W
                       - W_cw_pump_W
                       - W_sw_feed_pump_W

RO off, preheater off veya domain/infeasible/power-deficit sonucunda
preheater fiziksel olarak kapaliysa seawater feed pump dP/guc sifir kalir.

ANN inputlari:

Cf_kg_m3 notu:

  Cf_kg_m3, Practical Salinity degil, kg/m3 biriminde tuz konsantrasyonudur.
  Ham CMEMS Salinity_CMEMS_native minimumu yaklasik 37.62 olsa da TEOS-10

  20.0 <= T_RO_in_C <= 45.0
  38.5 <= Cf_kg_m3 <= 41.5

  Sistem kodu ANN inputlarini clamp etmez. Domain disi ise RO o saat icin

  Feasibility classifier:
    Models.Feasibility.Net ve Threshold ile P_feasible -> ClassifierFeasible.

  Performance regressors:
    W_RO_train_kW
    P1_opt_gauge_MPa
    P2_opt_gauge_MPa

  Hybrid Cp:
    Cp ActiveClassifier, Cp MarginRegressor ve CpLimit=500 mg/L mantigi.
    Boundary aktifse Cp=500 mg/L kabul edilir; aksi halde Cp=500-margin.

  Qp_train_m3h = Qf_train_m3h * R_target
  SEC_kWh_m3 = W_RO_train_kW / Qp_train_m3h

Bunlar ANN tarafindan ayri regresyon olarak egitilmez.

  Classifier infeasible dese bile:
    in-domain
    Cp boundary active
    Cp <= 500 mg/L + tol

  Ayni RO pump/booster/PX gucleri sistemde ikinci kez eklenmemelidir.
  N_train_running ile toplam guc:
    W_RO_total_W = N_train_running * W_RO_train_kW * 1000
  Toplam urun suyu:
    Qp_total_m3h = N_train_running * Qp_train_m3h


20. RO TREN DISPATCH ALGORITMASI
--------------------------------

Aktif fonksiyon:

  system_hourly_coupled_solver_v1 localDispatchRoTrains

Girdi:

  Ntotal = round(cfg.ro.N_train_total)
  TcwHotC = ORC condenser outlet
  WorcNetW = ORC net after solar pump deduction
  QcondW
  PHDesign, CTDesign, CTConfig
  orcCondDpCwPa = ORC condenser cooling-water dP
  previousNtrain

Candidate sirasi:

  Eski davranis dogrudan Ntotal, Ntotal-1, ..., 1 aramasiydi.
  Aktif davranis once previousNtrain varsa oradan baslar:

    startCandidate = clamp(round(previousNtrain), 1, Ntotal)

  Once startCandidate:Ntotal yukari yon denenir; bir reject gorulmeden once
  bulunan son kabul edilebilir aday bestDispatch olarak tutulur. Gerekirse
  sonra startCandidate-1:-1:1 asagi yon fallback denenir. previousNtrain yoksa
  startCandidate=Ntotal olur.

Algoritma:

  for nTrain in candidateOrder
      mdotSw = nTrain*Qf_train*rho/3600
      preheater rating/control
      RO ANN call for one train
      if domain fail: remember firstDomainFail, continue
      if ANN infeasible: remember firstInfeasible, continue
      W_available_lower_bound = WorcNetW
      if nTrain*W_RO_train > W_available_lower_bound:
          remember firstPowerDeficit, continue
      cooling tower fan solve
      CW circulation pump ve seawater feed pump hesapla
      W_available_for_RO = WorcNetW
                         - W_CT_fan
                         - W_cw_pump
                         - W_sw_feed_pump
      if W_available < nTrain*W_RO_train: remember firstPowerDeficit, continue
      accept largest feasible powered nTrain
  end

  Boylece ANN domain disi veya ANN infeasible adaylar icin CT hic
  cozulmez. Ayrica CT ve pompalar sifir kabul edilse bile RO gucu ORC netten
  buyukse aday CT cozulmeden reddedilir. Yalniz domain/gecerlilik ve guc
  lower-bound'u gecen adaylarda CT + pump hesaplari calisir.

Basarili kabul:

  RO_feasible = true
  ro_status = OK
  system_status = OK veya OK_CT_LIMITED

Hic aday kabul olmazsa oncelik:

  firstPowerDeficit varsa:
    system_status = POWER_DEFICIT
    ro_status = POWER_LIMIT_NO_TRAIN

  degilse firstDomainFail varsa:
    system_status = RO_DOMAIN_FAIL
    ro_status = RO_DOMAIN_FAIL

  degilse firstInfeasible varsa:
    system_status = RO_INFEASIBLE
    ro_status = RO_INFEASIBLE

Onemli fiziksel duzeltme:

  Hic aday kabul edilmezse secilen Dispatch, domain/infeasible/power-deficit
  adayinin preheater/CT durumunu sistemde birakmaz. RO fiziksel olarak off
  kabul edilir:

    N_train_running = 0
    W_RO_total_W = 0
    Qp_total_m3h = 0
    preheater_on = false
    Q_preheater_W = 0
    T_RO_in_C = raw seawater temperature

  Bu durumda ORC condenser heat, preheater'a phantom olarak aktarilmaz;
  TcwHotC dogrudan cooling tower hattina gider. Boylece RO_DOMAIN_FAIL
  saatlerinde 10-train veya benzeri aday debilerinden kalan sahte preheater
  duty, CT NOT_NEEDED ve CW return degerleri rapora sizmaz.

N_train_total < 1:

  system_status = NO_RO_TRAIN_INSTALLED
  ro_status = NO_RO_TRAIN_INSTALLED

   coldStream.T_in = current condenser-loop return

     W_orcnet
     Q_orcevap
     Q_orccond

     condenser water outlet -> preheater/CT/RO dispatch

   preheater_on is true.

   T_cw_after_preheater -> T_CT_out_C
   W_CT_fan_W subtracts from RO available power.
   makeup water subtracts from net product water summary.

8. Auxiliary pumps:
   ORC condenser cooling-water dP + active preheater hot-side dP ->
   W_cw_pump_W.
   Active preheater cold-side dP -> W_sw_feed_pump_W.
   Both subtract from W_available_for_RO_W.

9. RO:
   T_RO_in_C, Cf, Qf_train, R_target -> ANN.
   nTrain chosen by train dispatch.

10. Storage:
   Solar excess and return sink heat -> water tank placeholder.

11. Loop closure:
   T_orc_hot_return_C -> next solar inlet residual/guess
   T_CT_out_C -> next condenser inlet residual/guess

  ORC ve RO calisiyor fakat CT hedef sicakligi tam yakalayamadi. Sistem
  calismasi degraded operating condition olarak kabul edilir.

SYSTEM_OFF_SOLAR_OFF:
  Solar input useful heat yok veya solar guard kapatti. ORC/RO skipped.

ORC_OFF:
  Solar var gibi gorunse bile ORC feasible/power-positive dispatch bulamadi.

RO_DOMAIN_FAIL:
  RO ANN inputlari hard training/deployment domain disinda.

RO_INFEASIBLE:
  ANN domain icinde ama feasibility classifier/guard sonucunda infeasible.

POWER_DEFICIT:
  En az bir train icin ANN feasible olabilir; fakat ORC netten solar pump,
  CT fan, CW circulation pump ve seawater feed pump dusulduktan sonra kalan
  elektrik butcesi hicbir train'i beslemiyor.

NO_RO_TRAIN_INSTALLED:
  cfg.ro.N_train_total < 1.

CT_TARGET_NOT_MET:
  Eski sequential dispatcher'da sistem status olarak kullanilir. Kapali
  cevrim aktif yolda bunun yerine cogunlukla OK_CT_LIMITED kullanilir.

31. GELISTIRMEDE DIKKAT EDILECEK KRITIK KURALLAR
------------------------------------------------

1. ORC Stage-1 geometry frozen kalmali; annual/off-design rating geometriyi
   yeniden boyutlandirmamali.

2. RO ANN inputlari clamp edilmemeli. Domain disi saatler RO_DOMAIN_FAIL
   olarak kalmali.

3. W_RO_train_kW tekrar pompalarla double-count edilmemeli.
   Bu deger RO train icindeki RO pump/booster/PX elektrik tuketimini temsil
   eder. Sistem seviyesinde eklenen CW circulation ve seawater feed pump
   yardimci tuketimleri ayridir.

4. Solar pump power ORC netten dusulmeden RO dispatch yapilmamali.

5. CT fan, CW circulation pump ve seawater feed pump power RO available
   power'dan dusulmeli.

6. CT makeup water net product water hesabindan cikarilmali.

7. Syltherm800 default hot stream'dir. Water adapter yalnizca karsilastirma
   veya debug icin kullanilmali.

8. Preheater fixed geometry yillik runtime'da yeniden design edilmemeli.

9. cfg.preheater.enforce_cond_out_above_feed aktif edilirse ORC dispatch
   feasibility alani daralabilir; ORC_OFF nedenlerinde PREHEATER_COND_OUT_GUARD
   gorulmesi beklenir.

10. RO_DOMAIN_FAIL, RO_INFEASIBLE veya POWER_DEFICIT saatlerinde kabul
    edilmeyen adaylarin preheater/CT ciktilari sistem durumuna tasinmamali.
    RO off ise preheater off ve N_train_running=0 kalmalidir.

11. RO dispatch candidate akisi preheater'dan sonra once RO ANN/domain ve
    guc lower-bound kontrolu yapmalidir. Domain disi veya kesin guc yetmeyen
    adaylarda CT solver cagrilmamalidir.

12. timeStep_min < 60 ise input interpolation aktif olur. Toplam enerji/su
    integralleri dt_h ile yapildigi icin summary tutarlidir.

13. Eski klasorlerdeki dosyalar path onceligini bozmamali. system_paths_v1
    aktif klasorleri -begin ile ekler.

14. Bu teknik dokuman, model arayuzu veya hesap mantigi degistikce ayni
    commit/degisiklik setinde guncellenmelidir.
